Our History

Four Decades of Heritage

Nirupama & Rajendra have dedicated four decades to preserving, innovating, and sharing India's classical dance heritage while creating new pathways for the future. From the very first production Parampurush (1995) to landmark research in Deshi Marga Adavu (2021) and national recognition with the Sangeet Natak Akademi Award (2023), Abhinava continues to set benchmarks in excellence, community impact, and artistic innovation.

We are storytellers hailing from the land of the oldest oral storytelling tradition — Bharata (India). Our body of work is based on techniques derived from Natyashastra — a 5000-year-old classical text on dramaturgy. Over the years, our efforts to showcase the spectrum of Indian dance forms in all their grandeur has garnered admiration from over 7.5 million people across the world.

If you listen carefully, at the end you'll be someone else.

— Nirupama & Rajendra

Company

Artistic Excellence

Rooted in tradition and creative in presentation, Abhinava’s productions are known for their magical storytelling, breathtaking choreography, original music, and exquisite costumes — all created in-house.

We have collaborated with distinguished music composers, master musicians, designers, and writers who have brought their creativity and artistry into the unique works of Abhinava.

This excellence has earned the SNA Award from the Honorable President of India, Smt. Droupadi Murmu (Category: Creativity & Experimental Dance), conferred at Vigyan Bhawan, New Delhi.

Over the years, Abhinava has built a team comprising choreographers, producers, creators, dancers, teachers, researchers and curators.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

For first-time visitors — common questions about Abhinava Dance Company and Abhinava Dance School:

What is the age to start dance at Abhinava?

5 years for Bharatanatyam and 6 years for Kathak.

Do you offer online classes?

Yes. On special requirement basis. To know more write to learning@abhinavadancecompany.com.

Do you perform for corporate events?

Yes, ADC has presented successful concerts at high-profile corporate events like Godrej, Tata, Bosch, Infosys, IFQM and more. To book Abhinava’s shows for corporate events, contact info@abhinavadancecompany.com.

Can I watch performances without prior knowledge of classical dance?

Yes. Abhinava performances are designed to reach audiences with or without context to traditional dance — poetic storytelling, dynamic movement, and rich music create an immersive experience for every viewer.

What is Deshi Marga Adavu?

Abhinava's Research Department has introduced Deshi Marga Adavu (DMA) — a new vocabulary of 108 artistically crafted movements created by Smt. Nirupama Rajendra. DMA bridges the Deshi Adavus of Bharatanatyam with the Marga elements from the Natyashastra. Know more at Research (DMA).

Founding

Artistic Directors' Vision

To create a living tradition that honours the past while fearlessly embracing the future — through performance, education, research, and community celebration.

As founders of Abhinava Dance Company — one of India's most popular and cutting-edge production houses recognised worldwide — Nirupama & Rajendra have been instrumental in introducing innovation, creativity, enthusiasm and imagination to the realm of Classical Art.

They stand today as Artistic Directors with a vision of adapting traditional themes to classical media that will reach out to an audience that may or may not have context to traditional dance — with techniques from 5000-year-old scriptures to 21st century technological innovations.

Artistic Directors

Nirupama & Rajendra

Nirupama & Rajendra are one of India's foremost classical dancers, who hail from a family of educationists, artists and Harikatha Vidwans (traditional storytellers).

They are not only dancers, but also choreographers, teachers and storytellers. Their performances are based on Indian classical dance perfected over the years from learning in music, dance, literature and culture — some dating back to 5000-year-old scriptures.

Family & Artistic Background: Legacy of Harikatha vidwans from Purandara Dasa.

The audience has fuelled our passion for over 35 years and continue to inspire us to bring forth holistic and creative presentations for the world.
