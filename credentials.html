Awards & Honours

National · 2023
Sangeet Natak Akademi Award

We are supremely proud of our directors Smt. Nirupama Rajendra and T.D. Rajendra who were conferred with the Sangeet Natak Akademi Award 2023 from the Central Sangeet Natak Akademi, New Delhi.

Our honourable President of India, Smt. Droupadi Murmu, conferred the award in a special ceremony on 6th March at Vigyan Bhawan, New Delhi. India's highest honour in the performing arts — recognizing sustained work of the highest professional order through performances, teaching and scholarship.
