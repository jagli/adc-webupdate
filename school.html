Established in 1994, the internationally acclaimed training wing of the Abhinava Arts Centre offers a comprehensive education in Classical Dance. Recognized by the ICCR for excellence in preserving the Guru-Shishya Parampara method of training, our institution is a world-class facility where cultural education and traditional knowledge from legendary Gurus and Maestros are passed down with immense care. With students aged 5+ to 60+, the Abhinava Arts Centre conducts routine classes for students of all ages. No matter if you are a dance lover with a passion for learning, or a parent in search of holistic dance education, there is something for EVERYONE here at Abhinava.

Programs Offered

Comprehensive Training

Abhinava Dance School offers comprehensive training in Bharatanatyam and Kathak, rooted in the Natyashastra. Students master technique while learning to become polished performers, developing refined taste in classical music and literature.

The programme emphasises stage presence — dressing appropriately and carrying oneself with grace — alongside practical exposure to costumes, accessories, music, sound and lighting. Learners intern in productions, assist and lead rehearsals, and present work through both intimate in-house showcases and professional stage performances.

South Indian classical

Bharatanrityam

Hailing from the ancient temples of South India, Bharatanrityam encompasses the ancient codified techniques of movement and storytelling that are described in the centuries-old Natya Shastra. The revival of this knowledge is credited to legendary Guru and scholar Padma Vibhushan Dr. Padma Subrahmanyam. Students at Abhinava Centre receive comprehensive education in Bharatanatyam, developing fine skill in the technical and storytelling elements of the Classical South Indian form. Learning at the Abhinava Arts Centre is further enriched by Bharatanrityam instruction. Abhinava Dance Company receives international acclaim for the unique presentation of Bharatanrityam — a 5000 year old movement vocabulary that continues to win hearts in today's age.

North Indian classical

Kathak

With origins across North India, Kathak is a classical dance style characterized by powerful footwork, graceful spins, and expressive dramatic elements that are used in combination to tell a story. At the Abhinava Arts Centre, students are exposed to a diverse and extremely rich Kathak parampara. Not only do Abhinava dancers learn the practical content of different gems within the Kathak tradition, but careful attention is given to a strong theoretical education. Abhinava Dance Company's Kathak repertoire is widely performed across India and internationally, receiving universal commendation for unique and innovative presentations of Kathak that remain true to the form.

The classical text

Natya Shastra Karanas

The Natya Shastra, also known as the Natya Veda, is a 5000-year old classical text on dramaturgy, wherein the classical dance styles of India have their origin. This text is said to have been codified by the sage Bharata, and includes content on all aspects of performance: movement, stage design, practical content, even costuming. Within this text lies a unique movement vocabulary, known as the Karanas, that has been reconstructed and passed down by Dr. Padma Subrahmanyam. At Abhinava Arts Centre, students have the unparalleled opportunity to explore the movement and theoretical content of this ancient text.

Research in the school

Deshi Marga Adavus (DMA)

A novel creation in the space of classical dance by Guru Smt. Nirupama Rajendra, the Deshi Marga Adavus (DMA) represent the bridging of Bharatanatyam's foundational vocabularies with the Margi Karanas of the Natya Shastra. The accompanying terminologies have been given in Sanskrit by Padma Bhushan Shatavadhani Dr. R. Ganesh. This vocabulary represents the excellence in both research and dance performance that are hallmarks of Abhinava Arts Centre.
