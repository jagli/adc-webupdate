Student Scholarships

Aspiring Talent

We have been supporting several students. Abhinava Dance School provides scholarships for aspiring talented students who wish to pursue their learning at the Institute.

  1. T.V. Dwarakanath Scholarship Program
  2. Guru Smt. Sundari Santhanam Scholarship Program
  3. Dr Maya Rao Scholarship

Abhinava has facilitated scholarships for their students from:

  • Central Sangeet Natak Akademi
  • Karnataka Sangeetha Nrithya Academy
  • Swara Laya
  • R.N. Murthy Endowment
  • Mr. Ramanujam

To apply for a scholarship, kindly write to Learning@abhinavadancecompany.com

International
ICCR Recognition

Abhinava Dance School is endorsed by The Indian Council for Cultural Relations (ICCR) as an institute meeting the international standards for Guru-shishya training in dance. ICCR has opened scholarships for foreign students who wish to learn at the institution.
